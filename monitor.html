Model assumptions (edit in code)
· Footprints: OpenStreetMap building outlines (fitted as oriented rectangles); roads and paths from OSM centerlines; parking traced from satellite at 19z.
· Numbering (from placards): hallway 1 at the start end — west for 3101 and 3105, east for 3106, 3110, 3120, 3130 (3115 assumed east). Within a hallway each floor numbers the street-face pair first, then the back pair; within a pair the unit nearer the start end comes first. Garden floors on the 20s hold only the back pair, so 01/02 are the southeast and southwest garden units, 05 and 09 stack above 01.
· Hallways are open breezeways (concrete floor straight through, stairs inside, open both ends) with a deep brick-pier landing framing each opening on every level; the closet balconies step back inside that, the apartment walls step back again; 3101/3106 have their two east breezeways joined by a central passage.
· The end units of each building have deep recessed corner porches with brick piers (closet door on the porch back wall); other units have projecting balconies with the closet door beside them. Garden units on the 20s are on the back face.
· 20-unit buildings (3110/3120/3130) are built into the slope: two storeys on the parking-lot (north) face, three on the back. Units 1–2 of each hallway are the back-face garden units; floors 2–3 hold 4 each.
· Deviation = closet RH minus the median RH of closets on the same building face.
